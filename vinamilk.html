Approach

  • Company and industry analysis: business model, market position, competitive landscape.
  • Historical model: 2021A–2025A income statement, balance sheet and cash flow, plus operating metrics.
  • Forecast: 2026E–2030E integrated three-statement model.
  • Valuation: FCFF-based DCF with a WACC build, cross-checked against trading comparables and a dividend discount model.
  • Scenarios: bull, base and bear cases.

All figures use VAS data throughout, so the historicals and forecasts are on one consistent basis.
